## Why Property Listify has entered release convergence

Property Listify is entering a launch-readiness phase because isolated engine and feature work has accumulated without one authoritative integrated local product view. Launch decisions must now be based on complete journeys, explicit evidence, and controlled promotion rather than the apparent readiness of individual worktrees or screens.

## Launch objective

The objective is a sufficient, honest, and operable launch product: one whose accepted journeys are reviewable against an exact commit, whose material shortcomings have an explicit launch decision, and whose approved code can later be promoted through a controlled release sequence.

Launch sufficiency is not a claim that every future-state architecture, optional workflow, or engine ambition is complete. A journey may be launch-sufficient when its supported scope is clear, safe, and honest.

## What is paused and what remains permitted

Broad new feature, engine, and speculative implementation is paused. Work remains permitted only when it is a separately approved bounded slice that advances launch authority, security, data integrity, severe operational-risk containment, or an accepted launch-register decision.

Security, data-integrity, and severe operational risks are exceptions to the pause, but they still require a defined owner, evidence, scope, and approval. They do not create blanket authorization for unrelated work.

## Decision and implementation boundary

Edward has final product-approval authority. ChatGPT holds architecture, scope, review, and implementation-authorization authority. Codex is responsible for implementing approved bounded slices and returning technical evidence.

A finding, audit note, test result, or backlog entry does **not** automatically authorize implementation. Every implementation requires an approved bounded slice that names its owner, affected authority, validation, and rollback boundary.

Where optional functionality is unfinished, the launch decision may be to simplify it, hide it, defer it, use an honest placeholder, or provide a temporary manual operational fallback. None of those choices may misrepresent delivered behaviour.
