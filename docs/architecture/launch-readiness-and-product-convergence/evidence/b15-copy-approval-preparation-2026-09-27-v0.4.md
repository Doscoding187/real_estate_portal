## MVP privacy decisions incorporated into v0.4

1. Collection and processing are limited to what is required for accounts,
   professional/provider verification, listings and platform services,
   enquiries/leads, payments and billing, support, security/fraud prevention,
   and applicable legal/compliance requirements.
2. Property Listify will not sell users' personal information.
3. Sharing with production service providers is limited to what is needed to
   operate the service. The final notice must identify the actual relevant
   provider categories and providers from the accepted production configuration,
   including locations and safeguards where applicable; this schedule remains
   unresolved and no providers are guessed from substitutes or older material.
4. The notice provides a route for access, correction, deletion where no longer
   authorised or required to retain, objection where applicable, and withdrawal
   of consent where processing depends on consent. Privacy requests use the
   confirmed support route and exact subject line above.
5. Privacy complaints may first be sent to support; the route does not limit
   recourse to the Information Regulator.
6. Optional marketing permission remains separate from required account/service
   acceptance. Applicable electronic marketing must provide an appropriate
   unsubscribe/opt-out mechanism.
7. Necessary authentication, security and session cookies may be used and
   disclosed. Advertising/behavioural-tracking cookies are excluded from the MVP
   position. Optional analytics or similar cookies actually present in the
   production artifact must be identified and handled appropriately. The exact
   artifact schedule is still pending.
8. Registration must require applicable Terms acceptance and make the Privacy
   Notice available at the point of collection; optional marketing consent must
   remain separate. Rendered implementation proof remains pending.
9. Personal information will not be retained indefinitely. Retention is
   purpose- and category-based; finance/tax/legal records are kept for the
   applicable statutory period. Other information is deleted or de-identified
   when its purpose and lawful retention basis end.
10. The detailed category-level retention/disposal schedule must be reconciled
    with the governed B09 census so the notice reflects actual held data.
