# B15 customer copy for founder and legal review

**Draft only — not approved, not published, and not a payment invitation.**
This text translates the founder-approved B01 offer into a reviewable customer
document. Bracketed items require a factual answer or legal/accounting decision.
The current `/terms` and `/privacy` product pages remain placeholders until a
reviewed version replaces them and B18 verifies the rendered result.

## B. Launch Access terms — customer-facing draft

**Supplier and contact.** The supplier is Property Listify (Pty) Ltd,
registration 2026/702814/07, trading as Property Listify. [FOUNDER/LEGAL:

**Who buys.** Independent Agent access belongs to the approved independent
Agent. Agency access belongs to the approved Agency owner; a legitimate invited
member uses the Agency entitlement rather than buying a duplicate individual
entitlement. Developer access belongs to the approved Developer organisation,
not simply to a login or brand profile. Account/profile or organisation
approval is a platform gate, not a statement that Property Listify has
independently verified a professional licence or outside registration.

**What is supplied.** The paid offer covers the applicable Agent, Agency or
Developer listing, publication, discovery and lead-follow-up path described
on the product page. Publication remains subject to the product's identity,
content and moderation rules. Launch Access does not promise a number of
visitors, leads, responses, transactions, sales or rankings. [LEGAL: finalize
content rights, prohibited material, moderation notice and suspension process
against the actual application behavior.]

**Price, payment and start.** The total offer prices are R499, R999 and R1,499
respectively, subject to accountant confirmation of the current tax wording.
The customer requests an invoice, pays by manual EFT using its reference, and
submits proof through the private billing path. Proof or an invoice alone does
not create paid access. Finance verifies funds and matching ownership, amount
and reference before activation. A successful approval creates one intended
entitlement and starts its 90-day term. [LEGAL/ACCOUNTING: approve the final
tax invoice/receipt wording and customer-visible payment instructions.]

**Renewal and expiry.** There is no automatic renewal. A customer who wants a
new term requests and pays for another fixed 90-day term. A valid pre-expiry
renewal begins when the existing term ends; after expiry, the new term begins
on verified activation. When access expires, new paid publication and new paid
enquiry intake stop under the product rules. Existing legitimate leads,
records, identity, membership and private inventory remain available only as
the application's lawful retention and access rules permit. [LEGAL: confirm
exact post-expiry customer access and notice wording.]

**Payment exceptions.** If payment or proof cannot be matched, access remains
pending while finance requests correction. Underpayment does not activate
access. Overpayment and duplicate payments are reconciled manually and never
buy extra entitlement automatically. If funds were received but access was
not activated, the default is a full refund after payer verification, subject
to applicable law. After activation, a cancellation/refund/dispute request is
reviewed individually under the approved Terms and applicable rights; there
is no blanket no-refunds rule. [LEGAL/FOUNDER: complete request channel,
timing, evidence, statutory treatment and refund method before publication.]

**Enquiries and personal information.** Enquiries are routed to the authorized
listing or organisation workspace, or to Property Listify operations when
the platform explicitly has custody. The Privacy notice explains the data
involved. [LEGAL: confirm customer content, lead-use obligations, complaints,
applicable law and any remaining contractual clauses.]
