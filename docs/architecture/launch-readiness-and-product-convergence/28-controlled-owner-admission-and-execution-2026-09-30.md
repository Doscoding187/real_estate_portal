## Admission contract and review surface

`PAID_MVP_ADMITTED_OWNERS` is a startup-snapshotted, comma-separated list of exact `agent:<user-id>`, `agency:<agency-id>` and `developer:<organisation-id>` pairs. IDs must be positive safe integers in canonical decimal form. No wildcards, aliases, duplicate pairs or partial acceptance. Missing configuration closes admission; any invalid component closes the entire list. Parsing is bounded to 100 pairs and 8,192 characters; these defensive limits are not the recommended cohort size. Restart is required for reviewed configuration changes. Never put real controlled-account identities in committed configuration or customer-visible status.

Admission is additive to the accepted controls, not permission to activate: approved product/owner matching, absolute sales pause, expiring staffed window, complete verified funds, canonical invoice/payment locking and normal 90-day entitlement rules still apply. No schema, migration checksum, catalog or entitlement term changes.
