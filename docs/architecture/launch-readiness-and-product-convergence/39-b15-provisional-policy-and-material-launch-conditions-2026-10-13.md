## Applicable requirements and internal interpretation

POPIA section 14 ties retention to a necessary lawful purpose and requires disposal/de-identification when that basis ends. Section 21 requires a written security contract with an operator. Section 72 provides several transfer grounds; a relevant binding protection agreement is one. The account Terms checkbox is not blanket transfer permission. An EU SCC/DPF reference alone is not a South African adequacy finding; the actual scope, security, onward transfer and applicable contract must be assessed. Core service necessity must be specific to the particular flow, not merely cheaper hosting. These are internal requirements-based conclusions, not a verified-compliance certificate. [POPIA, sections 14, 21 and 72](https://www.treasury.gov.za/POPIA/POPI%20Act%204%20of%202013.pdf).

For ordinary account, enquiry and required service-email processing, the provisional route is the specific service purpose plus applicable provider security terms and a documented section-72 basis. Restrict full payment attachments and unnecessary identifiers; do not send them in service mail or analytics. Contract formation/security and foreign transfer are distinct questions. If the written security arrangement or a defensible transfer ground is missing, contain that flow until resolved; a generic consent banner does not remedy it.

AWS's [service terms, 1.14](https://aws.amazon.com/service-terms/) incorporate its [DPA](https://d1.awsstatic.com/legal/aws-gdpr/aws-gdpr-dpa-online.pdf). That DPA covers instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, incident assistance and region control, subject to stated exceptions. EU SCC applicability is conditional; it is not an automatic South Africa transfer exemption.

Railway's [DPA execution clause](https://railway.com/legal/dpa) explicitly requires its DocuSign process and company execution. Its public text describes security/confidentiality and onward subcontractor duties, but an unexecuted public template is not proof that the company has a binding agreement. Confirm execution and covered account/data subjects before personal-data launch; preserve the technical preparation and funding hold.

Their publication does not by itself prove this company's complete POPIA position. Microsoft’s [DPA index](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs/view/Microsoft-Products-and-Services-Data-Protection-Addendum-DPA?lang=1) identifies the subscription contract route; record the actual account coverage rather than inventing a signed document.
