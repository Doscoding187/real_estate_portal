## AAE-S0 relationship classification

Each atomic proposition records one of the following relationships:

- **Aligns** — applies or restates an approved AAE-S0 direction without introducing a material new proposition decision.
- **Extends** — adds evidence-bounded proposition detail needed by AAE-S1B without changing the approved AAE-S0 audience model, platform thesis or commercial boundaries.
- **Amends** — changes only an explicitly identified part of AAE-S0 through a recorded authority decision while retaining all unaffected AAE-S0 and S1A boundaries.
- **Supersedes** — replaces prior authority; no AAE-S1B proposition record is classified `Supersedes`.

This pack contains exactly two `Amends` records: `OWN-PROP-01` and `INV-PROP-01`. They amend only the AAE-S0 audience structure by recognising private property owners/direct listers and individual/portfolio property investors as distinct strategic audiences. No record supersedes AAE-S0 or S1A.

Customer-problem and pain-point statements remain strategic hypotheses unless supported by separately approved customer or market evidence. Repository capability evidence can support solution truth; it cannot prove how frequently a customer problem occurs.
