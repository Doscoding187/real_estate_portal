## Honest launch-stage narrative

For future launch-stage planning, the approved internal category narrative is:

> **Property Listify is building a South African property ecosystem that connects property discovery with the work that moves property opportunities forward.**

This is not approved launch copy and does not establish early-access status, public availability, commercial maturity or a commercial offer. For professionals and partners, the supporting internal interpretation is that Property Listify is designed to connect the presentation of property opportunities, demand capture and supported operational workflows with the next steps that move property decisions forward.

Release-matched product surfaces may later be demonstrated only where S1A identifies a qualified demonstration (`PRF-16`–`PRF-22`), and every demonstrated workflow must retain its delivered, qualified or future-direction classification.

The following distinctions remain mandatory:

| Term | Meaning | It does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability | A recorded repository-supported behaviour, classified delivered or qualified. | Production adoption, full end-to-end operation, commercial approval or entitlement. |
| Availability | A separately approved audience/route/operational state. | Plan inclusion, price, offer, trial or eligibility. |
| Commercial offer | A canonical, effective-dated package, eligibility and activation arrangement. | Current capability merely because code or browser content exists. |
| Product demonstration | Release-matched illustration of a supported workflow. | Customer result, customer identity, proof of demand, conversion, verification or commercial availability. |
| Future direction | Explicitly exploratory idea or foundation. | Current value, date, price or promise. |

## Product-demonstration doctrine

Real product demonstrations may be used in future experience work only when they are release-matched, audience-relevant and described as illustrations of supported workflow. A demonstration must be removed or re-reviewed when its source UI, route, data method, workflow contract or permissions change. It may not be paired with invented customers, testimonials, metrics, price, trial, verification or future capability language.

## Boundary between architecture and final expression

This document establishes a message hierarchy, not a page hierarchy. It deliberately contains no headline, slogan, CTA label, conversion copy, price, plan, offer, trial, SEO title or public route recommendation. Any later public expression must first pass proposition traceability, commercial-authority reconciliation, proof/legal review and implementation authority.
