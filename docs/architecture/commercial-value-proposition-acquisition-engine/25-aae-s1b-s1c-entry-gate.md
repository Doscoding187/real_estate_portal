## What a separately authorised S1C may rely on

S1C may use the recommended internal master proposition (`PLT-PROP-04`), the audience proposition records (`CON-PROP-01`–`CON-PROP-04`, `OWN-PROP-01`–`OWN-PROP-04`, `INV-PROP-01`–`INV-PROP-04`, `AGT-PROP-01`–`AGT-PROP-04`, `AGY-PROP-01`–`AGY-PROP-04`, `DEV-PROP-01`–`DEV-PROP-04`, `SRV-PROP-01`–`SRV-PROP-04`, `FIN-PROP-01`–`FIN-PROP-04`, `TDP-PROP-01`–`TDP-PROP-04`) and the four-layer model `PLT-PROP-06`, subject to their delivered, qualified, amended and hypothesis limits. It may treat `PLT-PROP-09` only as explicitly exploratory future direction and must retain `PLT-PROP-10` as a prohibition boundary.

The owner and investor records may inform later commercial decisions, but they do not establish direct-listing eligibility, ownership verification, publication rights, investor advice, yield or valuation methodology, portfolio operations, pricing, packages, routes or availability.

This is not approval of a plan, package, price, offer, eligibility rule, route, conversion journey, trial, entitlement, commercial availability or public statement.

## Commercial-authority reconciliation required in S1C

The future exercise must reconcile—not assume—`DEP-01`–`DEP-16`: shared plans and entitlements, agent entitlement/publication rules, agency billing, legacy subscriptions, developer/partner subscriptions, coupons, discounts, early access, browser-local advertising prices/trials, checkout/payment, cancellation/expiry and a public commercial read model. No canonical authority has been selected.

Public price, plan, offer, trial, discount, eligibility, entitlement-inclusion, activation, cancellation or billing statement remains blocked until that reconciliation has been separately reviewed and approved.

## Public-message and implementation boundaries retained

S1C may not treat any S1B proposition as permission to use the prohibited evidence in `PLT-PROP-10`, `PRF-01`–`PRF-15`, `PRF-23`–`PRF-28`, `PRF-30`, or `CLM-01`–`CLM-30`. It must not repair or publish the P0/P1 routes and claims in `RTE-01`–`RTE-13` without separate implementation authority.

Unassigned or required future owners remain: commercial authority; product analytics; marketing/legal claims; compliance; finance/legal; partnerships; data governance; sales handoff; and product owners for each audience. Their approval is required before statements in their domains become public.

## Gate rationale

`AAE_S1C_ENTRY=REQUIRES_FOUNDER_DECISION`

S1B provides a sufficiently bounded nine-audience internal proposition architecture to inform a future commercial-model exercise. However, the private-owner and investor additions introduce material decisions about identity, ownership authority, disclosures, publication, operating responsibility, regulated financial or investment claims, data methodology, portfolio scope and commercial participation.

Those decisions would materially shape revenue, eligibility, regulatory exposure, customer experience and delivery commitments. A founder decision must define S1C scope and priority before S1C is authorised. If separately authorised, S1C’s purpose is to reconcile commercial motions, offers, pricing and authority conflicts—not to assume they are already settled. AAE-S2 remains blocked until the resulting S1C architecture and public implementation boundaries receive review and approval.

## Re-entry requirements

Before authorising S1C, reviewers must approve a decision brief identifying the initial commercial audience priority, intended sales motion, commercial authority owners, legal/compliance review path and permitted operating assumptions.

The brief must explicitly address whether private-owner listing and investor participation are in the initial S1C scope; the proposed owner identity, ownership and publication authority; the investor acquisition-versus-operations boundary; financial and investment-claim governance; data methodology; and any partner or commercial model assumptions.

The brief must state whether it aligns with, extends, amends or supersedes AAE-S0, S1A or S1B authority.
