## Purpose

Property Listify is building a South African property ecosystem that connects how people discover property with how professionals and partners present opportunities, organise work and progress decisions. Consumers create the demand context through discovery and enquiry; they are participants in the ecosystem, not commercial subscribers or advertisers. The value model is **Discovery → Trust → Operations → Progression** (`PLT-PROP-06`).

This is a platform-category direction, not a statement that every workflow, audience journey or commercial product is already complete or available.

## Recommended internal proposition

> **Property Listify is building a South African property ecosystem that connects property discovery with the work that moves property opportunities forward.**

Candidate A is selected because it expresses the platform category and commercial ambition in understandable language while preserving launch-stage truth. “Is building” does not claim that the ecosystem is complete, commercially available or already adopted. “Moves property opportunities forward” describes the intended connection between discovery and supported next-step work; it does not promise a response, transaction, approval, conversion or completed outcome. Traceability: `PLT-PROP-04`.

### Business-facing internal interpretation

> **For professionals and partners, Property Listify is designed to connect the presentation of property opportunities, demand capture and supported operational workflows with the next steps that move property decisions forward.**

This interpretation supports later audience-specific proposition and commercial-model work. It is not approved public copy and does not establish product availability, plan inclusion, pricing, entitlement, onboarding or a conversion claim.

### Audience-structure amendment

AAE-S1B adds private property owners/direct listers and individual/portfolio property investors as distinct strategic audiences. This is a targeted amendment to the AAE-S0 audience structure because neither audience had a complete proposition context in the original seven-audience baseline.

The amendment does not change the selected master proposition or the Discovery → Trust → Operations → Progression model. Owner-specific availability and investor operating capabilities remain limited by their individual evidence and future-authority boundaries.

### Rejected boundary example

“One platform to complete every property journey” is not a candidate. It is rejected because it implies completeness, end-to-end fulfilment and availability beyond S1A evidence (`PLT-PROP-10`, `CLM-04`, `CLM-24`–`CLM-30`).

## Four-layer interpretation

| Layer | Internal role | Evidence-safe interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Help people find available property information and present supported opportunities. | Search/detail/location/Explore and eligible-publication foundations are qualified; public enquiry capture is narrowly delivered. |
| Trust | Make available information, identity and workflow context more intelligible. | Profiles and location context are qualified; professional, lead, listing and provider verification are not established. |
| Operations | Help participating professionals organise supported work. | Listing, lead, agency, development, provider and partner foundations are qualified; packaging/availability remain unresolved. |
| Progression | Help supported interest advance to an appropriate next step. | Enquiry/routing, viewing, deal, affordability and referral foundations are qualified; no response, approval or completion promise is supported. |

## Launch and wording boundary

Release-matched demonstrations (`PRF-16`–`PRF-22`) may illustrate supported workflow only. They cannot prove customer results, usage, commercial offer, entitlement, verification or traction. Public expression, routes, pricing, offers, trials, eligibility and implementation remain outside S1B.
